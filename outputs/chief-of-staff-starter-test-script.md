# Test Script — Chief of Staff Starter (Anna)
### Rebecca's pre-publish gate: verify the claims before they ship

> **The question we're answering:** Can a real first-time Ronnie go from paste to a usable
> sorted plan in **20 minutes or less**, without getting stuck? Until this is timed with real
> users, the "20 minutes" headline is **UNVERIFIED**. No claim ships without a source — this
> test is the source.
>
> **Owner:** whoever runs it · **Time needed:** ~30 min per tester · **Testers:** 3 minimum.

---

## Who to recruit (3 people)
Real Ronnies, not teammates who already get it. The closer to the buyer, the truer the result.
- **Tester A** — comfortable with tech, new to AI agents.
- **Tester B** — *not* techy, has avoided AI out of overwhelm. (This is the make-or-break one.)
- **Tester C** — busy professional juggling business + home (the core buyer).

Don't coach them. Hand them only what a real lead would get: the `chief-of-staff-starter.md`
file and "follow it start to finish." Then watch and time. **You stay silent unless they're
fully stuck for 60+ seconds** (and log it when that happens).

---

## Setup before they start
- Have them screen-share (in person or on Zoom) so you see where they hesitate.
- Start a stopwatch the moment they open the file. Stop it when Anna delivers the sorted plan.
- Have this sheet open to log timestamps and friction live.

---

## Timed run — checkpoints
Log the clock time at each checkpoint and note any hesitation.

| # | Checkpoint | Target by | Watch for |
|---|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | Found and opened Claude, created a Project (or chat) | 4:00 | Do they know what a "Project" is? Did they fall back to a chat? |
| 2 | Pasted the full prompt correctly | 6:00 | Did they grab the *whole* gray box, or miss lines? |
| 3 | Answered the 3 setup questions | 11:00 | The "genius zone" question — do they understand it, or stall? |
| 4 | Completed the brain-dump | 15:00 | Do they over-organize it (defeats the point)? Do they under-share? |
| 5 | Read the sorted plan + first-move draft | 20:00 | Does the output actually feel useful, or generic? |

**Total time to usable plan: ______ (this is the headline number.)**

---

## What to watch for (the panel's predicted sticking points)
These are the spots most likely to break. Mark each: ✅ smooth / ⚠️ hesitated / ❌ stuck.

- **"Project vs chat" (Zola's risk):** did Step 1 confuse them? ___
- **"Genius zone" question (Zola):** clearest source of stalls — did they get it? ___
- **Think-vs-act expectation (Albert):** did anyone expect Anna to *actually* send/order/
  schedule something and get confused when she drafted instead? ___
- **Brain-dump behavior (Rebecca):** did the example language ("permission slip," "mom's
  appointment") feel like *their* life, or off? ___
- **Trust moment:** when Anna labeled a guess `[my guess]` or asked "which agent first?" —
  did that build confidence or feel unfinished? ___

---

## After the run — ask each tester (2 minutes)
1. "On a scale of 1–10, how useful was what Anna handed back?" (target: 8+)
2. "Did anything make you feel dumb or behind?" (target: no — that's a brand-killer)
3. "Would you do the $27 workshop after this?" (the conversion signal)
4. "In your words, what does Anna do?" (tests whether the positioning landed)

---

## Verdict thresholds (decide before you look at results)
- ✅ **SHIP the "20 minutes" claim** if **2 of 3** finish in ≤20:00 *and* no tester rated
  usefulness below 7 *and* no tester felt dumb.
- ⚠️ **REVISE the number** (e.g., "in under 30 minutes") if testers consistently land at
  22–30 min — change the headline, then it's true again. A true 30 beats a false 20.
- ❌ **FIX before publishing** if anyone got truly stuck (❌) at the same checkpoint, or
  usefulness fell below 7. Patch that checkpoint's instruction and re-run with 1 fresh tester.
