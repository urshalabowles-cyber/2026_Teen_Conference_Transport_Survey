## Retention safeguards (don't skip)
- **Proof in the first 40 seconds** — the finished dual-output cold open is the whole hook.
- **The "this isn't for tech people" wall comes down by 1:40** — earlier than feels natural.
- **One hero agent, two worlds** — do NOT introduce a second agent on screen; the range comes
  from the *same* Chief of Staff doing business then personal. That's what keeps it focused.
- **Show one business win AND one personal win** — if you cut for time, cut narration, never
  cut one of the two builds. Losing either loses half the audience (the validator's #1 note).
- **Say "up to $150,000" and "eliminated the need to hire"** — it's avoided cost, kept honest.

## Voice reminders
Bold, warm, direct, premium. Fifth-grade clarity, college-level respect. Never condescend —
she has a degree and a career; she's new to LLMs, not to work. The money is not in the tool.
The leverage is in the team.
