# Example Run — what good looks like

This is a real example so you can see the kind of output the team produces before you run
it on your own business. The input was a coach helping high-performing Black women leave
corporate. Yours will be in your voice, about your business.

> **Input used (`MY-BUSINESS.md`):** Coaches mid-level corporate Black women, 35–50,
> exhausted by proving themselves, 12–18 months from wanting out. Sells a 90-day private
> advisory to design and launch their consultancy (~$3,000). Voice: direct, warm, premium,
> big-sister energy.

---

## 1. Positioning

For Black women who are excellent at their jobs and exhausted by proving it — I build the
exit plan, so you leave on your terms with a consultancy already earning, not a résumé and
a hope.

## 2. Core Offer

- **Who it's for:** high-performing Black women in corporate, 35–50, 12–18 months from leaving
- **Painful problem:** they're brilliant employees with no plan, no offer, and no system for the business they keep saying they'll start
- **Measurable outcome:** a positioned consultancy with a signed first client inside 90 days
- **Unique mechanism:** The Exit Plan — a 90-day private advisory that builds the offer, the proof, and the first sale *before* you resign
- **Why now:** every month inside is a month of leverage you're handing to someone else
- **Price band:** $3,000 (private, 90 days)

## 3. YouTube Angles

**Angle A**
- **Title:** "Don't Quit Your Corporate Job Until You've Built This"
- **Speaks to:** the fear of leaving without a safety net
- **Hook:** "The smartest thing you can do before you resign is build the one thing your job will never give you — leverage that's yours."

**Angle B**
- **Title:** "You're Not Burned Out. You're Under-Leveraged."
- **Speaks to:** the reframe from exhaustion to opportunity
- **Hook:** "What you're calling burnout is your skill outgrowing the place you're spending it."

**Angle C**
- **Title:** "I Help Women Leave Corporate With a Business Already Earning"
- **Speaks to:** the dream of a clean, funded exit
- **Hook:** "Imagine handing in your notice the same month your consultancy lands its first client. That's the plan."

## 4. Lead Magnet

- **Name:** The 90-Day Exit Map
- **Promise:** the exact sequence to go from "I want out" to "first client signed" — without quitting first
- **Format:** a one-page fill-in map (not a PDF you skim and forget)
- **3-section outline:** (1) Name your leverage — the skill people already pay you for ·
  (2) Package it into one offer you can sell this quarter · (3) Land proof before you resign
- **Why now:** it turns a vague someday into a dated, doable plan
- **Gap it leaves on purpose:** it shows the map; it can't tell you if *your* offer is priced
  and positioned to actually sell. That's the advisory.

## 5. Follow-Up Sequence

1. **Subject:** Your 90-Day Exit Map is inside — **Core idea:** deliver + first action;
   "off" feelings are the judgment gap, not a bug. **CTA:** download + reply with your leverage.
2. **Subject:** The map isn't the plan — **Core idea:** a map shows the route; your specific
   offer is the vehicle. **CTA:** book a fit call.
3. **Subject:** You're not behind. You're early. — **Core idea:** the belief shift — leaving
   well is built, not gambled. **CTA:** read how the advisory works.
4. **Subject:** She signed her first client before she quit — **Core idea:** representative
   result; the map got her moving, the advisory got her paid. **CTA:** see if you're a fit.
5. **Subject:** Last note on The Exit Plan — **Core idea:** plain close: what it is, $3k, 90
   days, who it's not for. **CTA:** apply for the advisory.

## 6. Validator Notes — what to fix before you publish

- **Strongest piece:** the positioning line. It's specific and it stings in a good way.
- **What would lose the buyer:** Angle B's title ("under-leveraged") is clever but may read
  abstract on a thumbnail — test it against Angle A, which is concrete.
- **The 3 judgment calls (yours):**
  1. *Niche too broad?* — "Black women in corporate" is close, but is it *all* industries or
     a lane you know cold (tech? finance? healthcare)? Narrowing raises the price.
  2. *Reads premium?* — $3,000 for 90 days private reads premium *if* the proof is on screen.
     Without a named result, it reads like every other coach. Add one.
  3. *Which hook leads?* — recommendation: lead with **Angle A** (concrete + fear-relief).
     But that's your call, you know your audience.
- **Fix this first:** add one real, specific client result. Everything else is sharp; the
  promise is currently a claim, not proof.
